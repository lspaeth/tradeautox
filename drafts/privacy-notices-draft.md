## Proposed new Terms section — §18 Privacy and transaction data

(Add after §17. Also add §18 to the §12 survival list.)

**We do not sell your information.** TradeAutoX will not sell, rent, or disclose your personal or business information to any third party, except as follows: (i) to the other member in a transaction you are part of, so you can complete your deal; (ii) to the service providers that operate the platform, including Stripe for payment processing and our hosting and email providers; and (iii) to authorities where required by law or to protect the platform and its members. We never share your information for anyone else's marketing, and we never share it with other dealers except the one you are doing a deal with.

**Transaction data.** TradeAutoX reserves the right to collect, retain, and use all buying and selling data arising from transactions between members on the platform — including vehicle details, listing and sale prices, bids, and the identities of the buying and selling dealerships — for operating the marketplace, preventing fraud, and improving the service. This data is not shared with any third party except as described above.

**Privacy Policy.** Our Privacy Policy at tradeautox.com/privacy explains in more detail what we collect, how we use it, and what we share. It is part of these Terms.
